Robotics, 3D Printing & Embedded Systems

Multi-Axis Articulated Robot Arm & Arduino Automation

A complete mechatronic robotic manipulator engineered in SolidWorks, manufactured using functional 3D printing (additive DFM), and controlled through an Arduino microcontroller architecture. Features multi-axis positional kinematic control, high-torque servo actuation, dedicated power bus routing, and a mechanical end-effector gripper.

Mechatronic System Architecture

3D Printing & Additive DFM Functional FDM components designed with specific print orientations to align layer lines with principal bending moments; calibrated bore tolerances for press-fit bearings and brass threaded heat-set inserts.
Electronics & Embedded Control Arduino microcontroller platform generating synchronized multi-channel PWM signals; dedicated external 5V/6V high-current power rail with bulk capacitor filtering to isolate inductive motor noise from logic circuits.
Kinematics & Joint Mechanics Multi-DOF articulated kinematic chain with dual-support clevis brackets, counterbalanced link geometry, and torque-reducing transmission mounts.

Manipulator Geometry & Mechanism Breakdown

FIGURE / 01 Master Arm CAD Assembly & Base Turntable

Complete 3D SolidWorks assembly of the articulated arm illustrating the base yaw rotation, shoulder joint, and elbow articulation. The base integrates radial ball bearing mounts to isolate axial thrust and overturning moment loads from the servo motor drive shaft.

  • Optimized Additive Geometry: Structural ribbing and hollow-core cross sections modeled to reduce moving link inertia, minimizing motor stall torque during rapid acceleration phases.
  • Fastener Architecture: Fastened using metric stainless steel socket head cap screws with counterbored seats embedded in the 3D-printed housing.

FIGURE / 02 Forearm Kinematics & Cable Management Channels

Secondary isometric projection highlighting forearm linkage positioning and internal wire pass-through conduits. Designed to prevent harness fatigue and cable binding during continuous rotational cycles.

  • Rotational Joint Clearances: 0.25mm diametral clearances maintained on all moving printed interfaces to ensure smooth motion without unwanted radial backlash.
  • Servo Horn Pocketing: Recessed spline pockets engineered directly into printed links for rigid, slip-free torque transfer from actuator splines.

FIGURE / 03 Parallel-Jaw End-Effector Gripper Mechanism

Precision dual-finger robotic end-effector driven by an integrated micro-servo through a spur-gear and parallel linkage mechanism. Converts rotary servo actuation into uniform symmetric linear jaw clamping.

  • Compliant Grip Geometry: Textured contact pads engineered to distribute normal contact pressure securely across varied payload shapes.
  • Gear Mesh Tolerancing: Custom involute tooth profiling compensating for FDM polymer thermal shrinkage to ensure smooth, bind-free gear mesh.

FIGURE / 04 Exploded View & Assembly Sequence

Isometric exploded layout detailing the bottom-to-top assembly sequence: cylindrical turntable base, twin-clevis shoulder joint, counterbalanced bicep link, forearm servo housing, and parallel-jaw end-effector.

  • Hardware Access: Sized and spaced for direct hex-key clearance during installation of M3 fasteners and servo horn retention screws.
  • Modular Maintenance: Modular joint clevises allow individual link replacement without disturbing adjacent wiring harnesses.

FIGURE / 05 Subsystem Architecture & Kinematic Axis Breakdown

Schematic breakdown of the manipulator illustrating each degree of freedom (θ1 to θ3) and actuation zone:

  • Base Turntable (θ1 Yaw): Deep-groove ball-bearing supported base isolating lateral overturning moments from the azimuth servo drive.
  • Shoulder & Elbow Clevis (θ2 Pitch): Dual-shear bracket pivots aligned with internal high-torque metal-gear servo horn pockets.
  • Forearm & Wrist Pitch (θ3): Lightweight counterbalanced link containing internal conduits to protect signal and power lines.
  • Parallel-Jaw Gripper (θ): Synchronized gear-driven linkage converting rotational servo stroke into balanced grasping force.

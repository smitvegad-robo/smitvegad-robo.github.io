5-DOF Bionic Hand & Embedded Actuation System

An integrated mechatronic prosthetic design featuring 5 independently actuated degrees of freedom (4 articulated fingers + 1 opposable thumb). Combines anatomical joint modeling in SolidWorks, additive manufacturing (3D printing), multi-channel PWM servo power regulation, and circuit simulation.

System Architecture & Engineering Domains

Mechanical & DFM Parametric phalange design, tendon-routing channels, flexure hinges, and additive manufacturing (FDM/SLA) tolerances.
Actuation & Kinematics 5 independent micro-servo drives (4 fingers + 1 thumb), tendon-driven linkage flexion with elastic return recoil.
Electronics & Controls Multi-channel PWM signal generation, isolated high-current 5V servo bus, flyback/ripple decoupling, and microcontroller logic.

Mechanical Assembly & Anatomical Kinematics

FIGURE / 01 Master CAD Assembly & Palm Packaging

Complete SolidWorks assembly demonstrating internal packaging of the 5 dedicated servo motors inside the palm housing. The chassis integrates anatomical joint pivots engineered to mirror human hand grasp kinematics.

  • Actuation Density: Compact palm bay layout positioning 5 independent servo horn sweeps without mechanical cross-interference.
  • Tendon Line of Action: Internal guide-tubes routed through the metacarpophalangeal (MCP) and interphalangeal (IP) joints for uniform bending torque.

FIGURE / 02 Multi-Grasp Articulation & Kinematic Envelopes

Kinematic modeling evaluating thumb opposition and independent finger curling trajectories. Sized for primary adaptive grip patterns: power grasp (cylindrical objects), precision pinch (index-thumb opposition), and spherical hold.

  • Thumb Circumduction: Angled knuckle boss providing 3D spatial opposition against all four opposing digits.
  • Joint Restraints: Integrated mechanical stops preventing hyperextension during rapid uncurling.

FIGURE / 03 DFM for Additive Prototyping & Pin Fits

Detailed view of modular segment joints, pivot pin press-fits, and weight-optimized hollow phalanges designed specifically for functional FDM/SLA 3D printing.

  • Fit Clearance: Tailored 0.3mm diametral clearances on joint pins to eliminate post-print friction without excessive radial play.
  • Weight Reduction: Ribbed internal cavities minimizing moving distal mass, reducing servo holding current and motor stall heating.

Electronics, Embedded Control & Circuit Simulation

FIGURE / 04 Microcontroller & Servo Drive Schematic

Complete electronics schematic detailing the control architecture. Displays dedicated PWM signal lines for each finger actuator, master logic rail isolation, and dedicated power routing.

  • Power Rail Isolation: Logic (3.3V/5V microcontroller) separated from inductive servo motor power lines to prevent voltage sag resets during stall surges.
  • Decoupling & Protection: Electrolytic buffer capacitance positioned across the servo bus to smooth current spikes during synchronous multi-finger grasping.

SIMULATION / 05 Dynamic Circuit Simulation & Signal Timing

Transient electronic simulation verifying multi-channel PWM duty cycle response, supply bus voltage stability, and signal integrity across concurrent 5-motor operational cycles.

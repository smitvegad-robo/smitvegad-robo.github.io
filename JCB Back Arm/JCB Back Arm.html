Heavy Machinery & Mechanism Design

JCB Back Arm Assembly & Linkage Modeling

Comprehensive 3D parametric CAD modeling, mechanical articulation layout, kinematic linkages, and structural fabrication drafting for a heavy-duty excavator backhoe boom and dipper assembly.

Engineering & Design Overview

CAD Platform SolidWorks (Parametric Assemblies & Weldments)
Subsystems Modeled Boom structure, dipper arm, 4-bar bucket tipping linkage, hydraulic pin clevises.
Engineering Focus Pin shear calculations, breakout torque, weldment layout, and clearance envelopes.

Technical Drawings, Assembly & Exploded Views

FIGURE / 01 3D SolidWorks Master Assembly

Complete 3D parametric model displaying the fully constrained excavator arm system. Accurately details the curved boom geometry designed to maximize trench depth and truck clearance while distributing cyclic digging loads through heavy-plate gussets.

  • Kinematic Mating: Concentric pin joints with defined limit mates modeling physical cylinder extensions and boom curl angles.
  • Pin Joints & Bushings: Sized for high surface bearing stress to withstand dynamic excavator breakout forces.

FIGURE / 02 General Assembly Blueprint & Orthographic Layout

Multi-view technical blueprint providing front, side, and top elevations of the articulated linkage. Illustrates overall physical envelopes, center-to-center pin distances, and ground-level reach bounds.

  • Geometric Layout: Verifies boom-to-dipper motion clearance preventing mechanical binding during complete curl strokes.
  • Hydraulic Packaging: Line-of-action alignment for boom and stick hydraulic rams.

FIGURE / 03 Detailed Fabrication Dimensions & Clearance Checks

Dimensioned engineering print highlighting critical fabrication measurements, pivot boss centerlines, flange thicknesses, and pin hole diameters.

  • Manufacturing Tolerances: Close-tolerance bored holes for press-fit bronze/hardened steel bushings.
  • Fabrication Geometry: Structural weldments with cut radii specified to minimize stress concentrations at joint junctions.

FIGURE / 04 Exploded View & Component Architecture

Isometric exploded view showing assembly sequence and disassembly hierarchy for field servicing, grease pin replacement, and cylinder pin rebuilds.

  • Assembly Hierarchy: Visual tracking of retention plates, cross-bolts, and thrust washers across all primary joints.
  • Maintainability: Direct fastener accessibility designed for field overhaul and maintenance tooling.

FIGURE / 05 Linkage Kinematics & Subsystem Breakdown

Detailed technical breakdown outlining load transmission across the 4-bar bucket tipping mechanism, moment arm variations through rotation, and cylinder rod mechanical advantage.

Mobile Commercial Soda Machine Cart

A custom commercial mobile vending kiosk engineered for a client using SolidWorks. Features a structural steel tubular weldment chassis (black frame) combined with modular, folded sheet metal body enclosures and protective canopy cladding (yellow panels) designed for weather resistance, equipment payload support, and streamlined workshop fabrication.

Engineering & Design Overview

CAD Platform & Tools SolidWorks (Structural Weldments, Sheet Metal, Multibody Assemblies)
Chassis Architecture Square hollow structural sections (SHS) with mitered corner joints, gusset reinforcement, and integrated equipment brackets.
Sheet Metal & Cladding Formed sheet metal roof panels and wall panels with hemmed edges, continuous drip channels, and fold tabs.

Technical Modeling, Weldment Layout & Orthographic Views

FIGURE / 01 SolidWorks Parametric Assembly & Feature Tree

Master 3D assembly showcasing full subsystem integration. Features a detailed feature manager tree with parametric relations linking the primary weldment framework to fold-out service counter extensions, dispensing valve stations, and overhead canopies.

  • Service Ergonomics: Workstation height, customer counter outreach, and beverage fountain head access configured for single-operator efficiency.
  • Modular Cladding Fastening: Rivet and fastener hole patterns aligned to weldment tabs to ensure clean alignment during final assembly.

FIGURE / 02 Isometric Assembly & Formed Sheet Metal Canopy

Isometric perspective highlighting the formed yellow roof canopy and upper side enclosures. The sheet metal panels are engineered with drainage contours and stiffening folds to maintain rigidity against wind loads and harsh weather without buckling.

  • Roof Ridge & Overhang: Extended rain deflection lip safeguarding service personnel, electronic controls, and beverage syrup racks.
  • Internal Storage Cavity: Lower compartment volume reserved for CO2 cylinders, refrigeration chiller units, syrup tanks, and power distribution.

FIGURE / 03 Structural Steel Weldment Frame (Chassis Backbone)

Dedicated structural weldment view of the load-bearing framework. Constructed using standardized square steel tubing with end-treatments, corner miters, and structural cross-braces designed to support dynamic transport vibrations and heavy liquid payload weights.

  • Weld Seam Strategy: Planned weld access points and stitch welding regions to mitigate thermal warp across long frame spans.
  • Structural Rigidity: Triangular corner gusseting engineered to resist torsional racking loads during curb transitions and rolling transport.

FIGURE / 04 Front Elevation & Dimensional Envelope

Orthographic front elevation illustrating maximum external transport envelopes, serving aperture heights, fold-out wing clearances, and ground wheel clearances.

  • Fabrication Flat Patterns: Derived 1:1 DXF flat pattern cutouts generated for laser cutting and CNC press-brake bending.
  • Standardized Cut-List: Tabulated structural weldment member lengths for zero-waste raw stock tube cutting.

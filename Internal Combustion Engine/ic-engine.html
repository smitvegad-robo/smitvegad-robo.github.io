IC Engine Mechanism & Subsystem Assembly

Comprehensive parametric CAD design, kinematic mechanism assembly, and multi-view technical drafting of a 4-stroke internal combustion engine subsystem developed in SolidWorks.

Design & Simulation Overview

CAD Platform SolidWorks (Parametric Parts, Subassemblies & Drafting)
Subsystems Designed Cylinder block, piston assembly, connecting rod, and counterbalanced crankshaft.
Engineering Focus Geometric dimensioning, running clearances, split-bearing fits, and DFM.

Technical Drawings & Kinematic Media

DRAWING / 01 Full Subassembly Layout & Sectional Projections

Complete multi-view technical drawing displaying front elevation, side profile, and top view projections of the assembled cylinder block. Features a full longitudinal section view displaying internal clearances, cylinder wall wall-thickness ratios, and valve/manifold packaging geometry.

  • Key Feature: Dimensioned overall envelope ensuring accurate engine mount alignment and chassis clearance.
  • Clearances: Verified mechanical interference margins between cylinder bore and valve guides.

DRAWING / 02 Piston Assembly & Crankshaft Geometry

Detailed engineering drawing focusing on critical reciprocating and rotating components: the piston crown, compression ring grooves, wrist pin bore, connecting rod with split bearing cap, and counterweighted crankshaft.

  • Piston Crown & Ring Pack: Toleranced ring-groove clearances engineered to accommodate thermal expansion under combustion cycles.
  • Split Journal Bearing: Connecting rod bottom cap designed with split-bearing alignment surfaces and high-tensile fastener spacing.
  • Crankshaft Web: Counterweight profiles modeled to offset primary reciprocating inertia forces.

DRAWING / 03 Exploded View & Bill of Materials (BOM)

Isometric exploded view mapping the complete bottom-to-top assembly sequence. Each discrete component is indexed with callout balloons matched directly to the structured parts list.

  • Disassembly / Assembly Flow: Visual confirmation of fastener accessibility and tool clearance during maintenance.
  • Structured BOM: Item numbers, component nomenclature, quantities, and material classifications for seamless manufacturing handoff.

SIMULATION / 04 Kinematic Motion Study & Assembly Verification

Dynamic SolidWorks motion simulation verifying slider-crank kinematics. Validates continuous 360° crankshaft rotation, smooth angular oscillation of the connecting rod, and linear reciprocating translation of the piston within the cylinder bore without mechanical bind or interference.

🔒 Manufacturing Data & Native Models

Native SolidWorks part trees (`.SLDPRT`), master assembly files (`.SLDASM`), and universal `.STEP` models with complete tolerance schemes are securely archived and available upon direct request.

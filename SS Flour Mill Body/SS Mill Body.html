Sheet Metal Design & Precision DFM

Stainless Steel (SS) Flour Mill Housing & Enclosure

Full sheet metal design and manufacturing engineering of a stainless steel domestic/commercial flour mill cabinet. Designed specifically for CNC laser profile cutting and CNC press brake forming, incorporating bend deduction deductions, integrated thermal ventilation louvers, and precision component mounting interfaces.

Engineering & Manufacturing Overview

Material & Fabrication Process Stainless Steel (SS 304 / Food Grade), CNC Fiber Laser Cutting, CNC Air Bending.
DFM & Bend Tolerancing Custom K-Factor and bend deduction tables matched to tooling V-die widths to guarantee zero-gap seam alignment.
Thermal & Equipment Packaging Integrated ventilation louvers for electric motor convective cooling, vibration-damped motor base mounts, and hopper throat flange.

Enclosure Geometry & Fabrication Features

FIGURE / 01 Front Isometric Assembly & Component Mounts

Complete isometric view of the formed stainless steel casing. The structure uses self-locating bend flanges and hemmed safety edges to provide superior rigidity while housing the high-RPM electric motor and milling chamber.

  • Laser-Cut Apertures: Precision-pierced bolt circles and service cutouts allowing direct fastening of internal grinder chambers and electric motor mounts without secondary manual drilling.
  • Corner Reliefs & Seams: Tear reliefs and corner notches calculated to prevent material bulging and distortion during sharp radius bends.

FIGURE / 02 Rear Enclosure & Ventilation Airflow Layout

Rear isometric perspective showcasing the integrated cooling pattern and access panel framing. Optimized to balance air exchange with dust ingress containment.

  • Convective Air Ventilation: Louver/perforation slot arrays aligned directly with the electric motor's axial cooling fan to dissipate continuous thermal build-up during extended milling runs.
  • Fastener Optimization: Recessed flange tabs accommodating flush clinch nuts (PEM nuts) and blind rivets for clean, food-safe exterior surfaces.
